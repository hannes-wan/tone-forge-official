**Tone Forge Community Edition is free to download and use.**

Tone Forge 0.1.0 introduces a free Community Edition for editing and auditioning modular synthesizer patches.

- Connect oscillators, filters, envelopes, gain, and effects in a native patch graph.
- Trace audio and modulation routes, then audition edits while shaping the sound.
- An effective license enables saving and reopening sound files and working with a sound library.
